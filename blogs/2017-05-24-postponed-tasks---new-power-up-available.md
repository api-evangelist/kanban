<p>In the spirit of strengthening your procrastination tendencies, an option to have a task postponed has just been added to Kanban Tool! :)<br />
Seriously, though - this is a fabulous way to de-clutter your board from tasks that you cannot get to work until a specific event takes place, or until you have the time for it.</p>

<p>The supported options are to postpone until the <strong>evening</strong>, <strong>next day</strong>, <strong>next week</strong>, a <strong>specific time and date</strong> or to an <strong>undefied</strong> point in the future (the dreamy sounding "<i>someday</i>" option).
When the set time comes, your task will come right back into the column, that it was set to delay from.</p>

<img alt="Postpone a Task Menu" src="https://static.kanbantool.com/blog/postpone-a-task-menu.png" title="" />

<p>You can also review all your work suspended in this way from the <strong>Postponed Tasks</strong> widget, displayed in the side panel, along with any other widgets you may be using (Calendar, Recurring Tasks, Team Activity, My Work).<br />
You are welcome to edit the schedule for the postponed items from here - or, if need be, bring a deferred task back to the board before the set time comes. Editing the task contents is also possible while suspended - just click the task from the Postponed Tasks widget and make your changes.</p>

<p>Enjoy being even better organized with this handy feature!</p>
