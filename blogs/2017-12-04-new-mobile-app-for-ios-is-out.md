<p>We're delighted to announce that our newest iOS application has been released.<br />
Simply update the app on your iPhone if you've used it before, or <a href="https://itunes.apple.com/us/app/kanban-tool/id1096506294">download it now</a> to use Kanban Tool on the go.</p>

<p>This update introduces a brand new, lovely interface as well as support of a number of new features.<br />
Navigating through the workflow stages has been made easier and you can now view task comments, checklists and history. All of the newest Power-Ups got mobile support too - you're welcome to use Task Summary, Recurring Tasks, Postponed Tasks and Checklist Templates on the go.</p>
