Have you ever wondered how come some people have no difficulty grasping complex ideas in an instant, while others need a lot of explaining and navigating through other associated concepts before they know what is being talked about? One might say this is simply a matter of IQ – and perhaps it is. But there are ways of tricking the way you think and think about thinking (!), resulting in broadening your understanding abilities and therefore allowing you to take more in and – in turn – give more back.
